About

Personal study tool for cardiologists. Visual style calibrated to ESC guideline figures so your brain maps them as one memory.

  • Local only. All data is in this device's localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Three modes. Algorithm-order, label-drag, piece-puzzle.
  • Anchors. Pin steps you know cold; rebuild the ones you don't.
  • Save & reuse. Every chart persists between sessions.

ESC guideline figures are © European Society of Cardiology. This app uses an ESC-style visual system built from scratch; the templates are your own.
